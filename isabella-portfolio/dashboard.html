Example dashboard · Power BI style

Athlete monitoring, one screen.

At Scottish Rugby I built Power BI reports that turned GPS, jump testing and wellness data into decisions for coaching, medical and S&C staff. That data is confidential, so I've recreated the reporting style here for Bath Lacrosse.

ⓘSample data. Players are anonymised and every number below is simulated to show the report design and interactions. It does not contain real Scottish Rugby or Bath Lacrosse data.

Bath Lacrosse Athlete Monitoring Women's 1st team · 2025 - 26 season · Sample data

Built for the reader

KPIs follow the order staff ask questions: did players check in, are they ready, what load did they do, how are they jumping, who is unavailable.

Click to filter

Like Power BI, every view cross-filters. Click a position bar or a session type in the donut, or use the slicer to drill into one player.

Flags, not just charts

The acute:chronic ratio panel and readiness heatmap surface the players who need a conversation before the next session.
